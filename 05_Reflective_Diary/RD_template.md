# Individual Reflective Diary

**Name:** _[Your name]_
**Module:** _[Module name / code]_

> This diary uses the **Gibbs Reflective Cycle** (1988). For each week, work through the six stages in order. Replace the prompt text under each heading with your own reflection — a few sentences per stage is usually enough. Copy the **"Week template"** block below for each new week.  Feel free to style how you want as long as the basic structure remains.

---

## Week 1 — Week commencing: _[9th March 2026]_

### 1. Description
*What happened? Describe the situation or experience factually, without judgement.*

_[Write here]_

### 2. Feelings
*What were you thinking and feeling before, during, and after?*

_[Write here]_

### 3. Evaluation
*What was good and bad about the experience? What went well, and what didn't?*

_[Write here]_

### 4. Analysis
*Why did things happen the way they did? What sense can you make of the situation? Link to wider ideas, reading, or theory where relevant.*

_[Write here]_

### 5. Conclusion
*What did you learn? What else could you have done?*

_[Write here]_

### 6. Action Plan
*If it happened again, what would you do differently? What will you carry forward?*

_[Write here]_
